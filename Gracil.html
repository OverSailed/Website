About Gracil

Gracil is Schelin Cruiser No. 16, a classic Swedish sailing yacht built in 1971. With a length of approximately 10.5 metres, a narrow 2.7-metre beam and a shallow-draft keel, she reflects the elegant proportions and practical offshore character of Scandinavian yacht design from her era.

During her life, Gracil has been modified with an additional mizzen mast and a bowsprit, giving her a distinctive appearance and sail plan.

Today, she is undergoing an extensive restoration covering the hull, keel structure, engine, electrical installation and interior. The aim is to preserve her original character while improving reliability, safety and suitability for longer offshore sailing.

About Gracil

Gracil is Schelin Cruiser No. 16, a classic Swedish sailing yacht built in 1971. She is approximately 10.5 metres long with a narrow 2.7-metre beam and a shallow-draft keel, giving her the slender proportions characteristic of Scandinavian yachts of her era.

Over the years, Gracil has been equipped with a mizzen mast and bowsprit, creating a distinctive sail plan and appearance.

Today, she is undergoing an extensive refit covering the structure, machinery, electrical installation, plumbing and interior. The aim is to preserve her original character while improving reliability and preparing her for longer offshore voyages.

Restoration

Hull & Keel

Structural refit

Inspection and repair of the hull and keel structure, including keel attachment, underwater fittings and protection of the laminate below the waterline.
