About Gracil

Gracil is Schelin Cruiser No. 16, a classic Swedish sailing yacht built in 1971. With a length of approximately 10.5 metres, a narrow 2.7-metre beam and a shallow-draft keel, she reflects the elegant proportions and practical offshore character of Scandinavian yacht design from her era.

During her life, Gracil has been modified with an additional mizzen mast and a bowsprit, giving her a distinctive appearance and sail plan.

Today, she is undergoing an extensive restoration covering the hull, keel structure, engine, electrical installation and interior. The aim is to preserve her original character while improving reliability, safety and suitability for longer offshore sailing.
